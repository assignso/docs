# Color and typography

## Palette

| Color | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oil black | `#0c0c0c` | Primary dark mark and dark surfaces |
| Pure black | `#000000` | Existing monochrome favicon and mark variants |
| White | `#ffffff` | Light surfaces and reversed marks |
| Deep blue | `#00075D` | Restrained emphasis |

[Download the palette JSON](/branding/colors.json).

Prefer a monochrome mark on a quiet, solid surface. Use deep blue sparingly. Do not recolor the mark to match a partner's palette or use brand colors as a substitute for meaningful status labels.

Check contrast against the actual background, including in dark themes and over imagery. A color's presence in this palette does not establish that every pairing is accessible. See [usage and accessibility](/branding/usage#contrast).

## Typography

Keep the Assign name readable in the surrounding application's or publication's established type system. Preserve the existing semibold treatment and tight spacing when reproducing an existing product lockup; do not imitate the lettering by modifying the symbol.

The documentation uses Geist for prose and Geist Mono for code. That is a documentation choice, not a requirement to install those fonts in every use of the brand. Existing Docs logo text uses its supplied Arial/sans-serif treatment.

Do not redraw a custom wordmark or distribute font files as part of an asset download. Obtain any required font rights separately.

## Voice

Use **Assign** with its initial capital. Write directly and calmly, with familiar words and clear actions. Describe what someone can do or what happened. Keep promotional language out of instructions, validation and error recovery.
