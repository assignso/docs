# Delete your Account <Badge type="warning" text="Upcoming" />

This workflow is not released yet. These instructions describe the upcoming Account deletion flow.

Open **Account settings → Security → Delete account**. Review the consequences, authenticate again
if requested, and enter **DELETE** to confirm. Assign returns a deletion request with a scheduled
date seven days later. Keep that acknowledgement; an interrupted request does not prove success.

On mobile, **Recover original action** checks an interrupted request using the original action.
Refresh the deletion status before making another change. After acknowledgement, **Sign out on
this device** clears local sign-in data without cancelling your deletion request. If you have no
Workspace selected, **Delete account** is also available on the Workspace chooser.

You can sign back in and explicitly cancel before the scheduled date. Signing in alone does not
cancel deletion. A request blocked by an ownership or billing prerequisite can also be cancelled.

Transfer ownership of every Workspace you own, including archived Workspaces, or complete its
deletion first. Finish or cancel any pending Workspace checkout. Account deletion does not cancel
a shared Workspace subscription, issue a refund, or delete shared Tasks, Documents and comments.

OAuth applications you own are disabled when your Account is erased.

After processing, your sign-in credentials and personal profile are removed and your memberships
are detached. Shared history keeps neutral attribution. Reserved usernames, justified billing and
security records, and backups follow their retention rules; deleting an Account is not a promise
that every backup disappears immediately. Profile images use the normal storage cleanup process.

Online deletion is not available yet. This page will provide the supported self-service link
when that workflow is released.
