# Delete your account <Badge type="warning" text="Upcoming" />

Account deletion is being prepared for release. The steps below describe the upcoming experience.

## Request deletion

1. Open Account settings, then **Security**. You can also use the [account deletion page](https://assign.so/delete-account) without opening a Workspace.
2. Sign in if asked. You may need to sign in again to confirm your identity.
3. Choose **Request account deletion**, read what will be kept, and type `DELETE` to confirm.
4. Check the receipt for your deletion deadline.

Your account stays usable during the seven-day cancellation window. Signing in does not cancel the request. Repeating a pending request does not move its deadline.

In the native app, open **Settings**, then **Delete account**. The profile menu also has a **Delete account** entry. If you have no Workspace, use **Delete account** on the Workspace chooser.

## Cancel or check the status

Return to **Delete account** to see the latest receipt, including after signing in again. Choose **Cancel deletion** before the displayed deadline to keep your account. If an action loses its connection, choose **Recover request** or **Recover cancellation** to check its original result and refresh the current status.

Once the cancellation window ends, the page shows that deletion is due. **Check status** retrieves the latest result.

## Resolve a blocked request

You must transfer ownership of your Workspaces or finish deleting them before deleting your account. Archived Workspaces still count. You must also finish or cancel a pending Workspace checkout.

Assign checks these conditions again when deletion is due. If they prevent deletion, your account stays active and the request shows **Blocked**. Resolve the blocker, then choose **Request deletion again** to start a new seven-day window.

## What deletion keeps

Deletion removes your personal profile and sign-in credentials and detaches your Workspace memberships. Shared Workspace content and records required for audit, financial, security or legal retention remain under their retention rules. Historical contributions can retain a neutral deleted-member attribution.
