# Privacy and cookie choices

Assign's public pages and blog use necessary storage to remember your cookie
choice. Google Analytics cookie storage is disabled until you accept analytics
cookies.

Analytics and the cookie-consent interface are not loaded in the authenticated
product, Workspace creation, settings, administration, or lab areas. These
private areas are excluded by path prefix; other current and future public pages
are included by default.

The consent banner offers two choices:

- **Accept analytics** allows Google Analytics to measure visits and page
  interactions so we can understand which pages and articles are useful.
- **Reject analytics** keeps analytics storage disabled. Google may still
  receive cookieless Consent Mode pings for basic measurement and modeling.
  Advertising storage, advertising user data, and ad personalization remain
  disabled in either case.

You can change your choice later with **Cookie preferences** in the website or
blog footer. Withdrawing analytics consent removes matching first-party Google
Analytics cookies such as `_ga` and `_ga_*` where the browser permits it.

The necessary `assign_cookie_consent` cookie records your selected categories,
the consent-policy revision, consent timestamps, and an identifier generated by
the consent tool. It lasts for up to 182 days and is shared across Assign's
`assign.so` subdomains so the website and blog can respect the same choice.

On eligible public production pages, Google Tag Manager uses advanced Google
Consent Mode with analytics and advertising storage denied before the container
loads. While storage is denied, Google says consent-aware tags may send
measurements without cookies, including browser-supplied fields such as
timestamp, user agent, referrer, consent state, and a random number generated
for the page load. Ads-data redaction is enabled, and the application does not
add private Workspace content, customer-entered content, or explicit identity
fields to these events.

Local development and browser tests do not load the production containers. If
analytics or the consent tool fails to load, the site and blog remain usable and
optional storage stays denied.
